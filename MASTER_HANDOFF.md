## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Identity

### 1.1 True Scope & Identity
**Autistic Scholars** is an independent research organization producing theoretical and empirical work on cognitive regulatory architecture and its consequences across biological, institutional, and social systems.

The organization's foundational epistemological insight is that **contemporary psychology and psychiatry have implicitly adopted the cognitive architecture of majority social interaction as an unexamined normative baseline**. By treating one specific regulatory strategy as the unmarked standard of human social functioning, psychological taxonomies render alternative cognitive architectures visible exclusively as deficit, disorder, or pathology.

Autistic Scholars makes this normative baseline visible, dissects its mechanics, and examines the multi-scale consequences of sustained cross-architectural mismatch.

### 2.1 Level I: Architectural Analysis
* **Title:** *The Other Side of the Double Empathy Problem: Making Majority Social Cognition Visible as a Specific Regulatory Architecture*
* **Author:** Ericwilliam Brown
* **Status:** Submitted for publication | **Preprint:** [PsyArXiv (DOI: 10.31234/osf.io/x9hs2_v1)](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/x9hs2_v1)
* **Core Question:** *What are the two architectures?*
* **Theoretical Contribution:**
  * Synthesizes predictive processing with Milton's (2012) Double Empathy framework.
  * Formalizes two stable regulatory organizations of social cognition:
    1. **Socially Coupled Cognition (SCC):** The statistical majority style. Stabilizes interpretation through high precision-weighting of shared social priors and distributed affective alignment.
    2. **Internally Mediated Cognition (IMC):** The autistic cognitive style. Stabilizes interpretation through elevated precision-weighting of bottom-up sensory prediction errors and internally coherent explicit models.
  * **Mathematical Formalization:** Defines the **Architectural Ratio** $\omega = \frac{\pi_p}{\pi_s}$ (relative precision-weighting of prior expectations vs. sensory prediction errors) and **Architectural Distance** ($\Delta\omega$) between dyadic interactants.
  * Reframes autism diagnosis as a **perspective-dependent observation** rather than an intrinsic impairment.

### 2.2 Level II: Computational Formulation
* **Title:** *Metabolic Gating in Social Interaction: An Energetic Account of Asymmetric Adaptation*
* **Author:** Ericwilliam Brown
* **Status:** Under Review | **Preprint:** [Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19098264)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19098264)
* **Core Question:** *How do energetic constraints shape the dynamics of social inference?*
* **Theoretical Contribution:**
  * Translates predictive processing gain control into biophysical energetics: maintaining high neuromodulatory sensitivity to interpersonal discrepancies is physiologically expensive.
  * **Metabolic Gating:** When an interactant reaches their metabolic sustainability threshold, their cognitive system downregulates sensitivity to conserve energy.
  * **Dyadic Energetic Asymmetry (DEA):** The moment one partner gates, the entire burden of interactional coordination is shifted onto the non-gating partner, creating an extraction of metabolic resources and unrecoverable energy debt.
  * Specifies empirical, falsifiable predictions for pupillometric, autonomic, and behavioral divergences over time.

### 2.3 Level III: Experiential & Phenomenological Analysis
* **Title:** *Structural Insolvency: Metabolic Sovereignty and the Bio-Political Economy of Autistic Burnout*
* **Author:** Ericwilliam Brown
* **Status:** Under Review
* **Core Question:** *What does this cost the body?*
* **Theoretical Contribution:**
  * Rejects clinical models that conflate autistic burnout with clinical depression or resilience deficits. Burnout is a **hardware crash** resulting from **Structural Insolvency** (sustained metabolic extraction exceeding replenishment capacity).
  * Integrates the bioenergetic vulnerability of **parvalbumin-positive (PV+) interneurons** with the **BIAPEM hypothesis** (*BOLD Increases Approximate Prediction Error Metabolism*).
  * Calibrated through the **Systemic Witness** positionality: documents the compound prediction tax of masking, racialized illegibility, and enforced double consciousness.
  * Connects chronic compensatory effort to **somatic weathering** (explaining the 16–30 year life expectancy disparity in autistic populations).
  * Posits **monotropism** as an essential negentropic defense strategy; demands **Metabolic Justice**, the right to opacity, and clinical auditing of systemic energy debt.

### 2.5 Level V: Ecological Dynamics
* **Title:** *Rethinking the Category Fallacy: Environmental-Relational Trauma and the Institutional Production of Distress*
* **Author:** Ericwilliam Brown
* **Status:** Submitted for publication
* **Core Question:** *How do institutional environments produce distress, and how can we trace structural relationships between institutional conditions and physiological consequence?*
* **Theoretical Contribution:**
  * Extends Arthur Kleinman's transcultural psychiatric critique of the **category fallacy** to environmental and institutional architectures.
  * Introduces the **Environmental-Relational Trauma (ERT)** framework: distress is produced when non-encoded cognitive architectures are held within environments characterized by:
    1. **Structural Incompatibility:** Environments calibrated exclusively to normative majority processing.
    2. **Constrained Exit:** Inability to leave (compulsory schooling, workplace survival, healthcare dependency).
    3. **Adaptive Concealment & The Concealment Loop:** Masking distress hides the mismatch from the institution, leading the institution to withhold accommodations, forcing further concealment and exponential allostatic load.
  * Replaces individual pathologization with institutional architecture auditing.

### 4.2 Typography
* **Serif Headings & Body:** `'EB Garamond', Georgia, serif`
  * Weights: 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semi-bold), italic.
  * Used for: Article text, headings (`h1`–`h4`), blockquotes, institutional title small-caps.
  * Base size: `html { font-size: 18px; }`, `body { line-height: 1.75; }`.
* **Sans-Serif System & UI:** `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`
  * Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700.
  * Used for: Navigation links, kickers, buttons, metadata labels, status chips, breadcrumbs.

### 4.3 Typographic Ornaments & Section Dividers
Adhering to traditional academic and humanistic publishing standards:
* `‡` (Double Dagger): Standard academic citations.
* `⁂` (Asterism): Research program structural dividing points.
* `❦` (Fleuron): Humanistic, biographical, and epistemological breaks.
* `·` (Interpunct): Navigation and inline metadata separation.

### 4.4 Accessibility & Ergonomics
* **Skip Navigation:** Skip link (`.skip-link`) present as first body child on every page.
* **Scroll Indicator:** `#scrollProgress` reading bar pinned to top of viewports.
* **Dark Mode:** Toggled via `#darkModeToggle`, persistently stored in `localStorage.getItem('darkMode')`.
* **Diagram Lightbox:** Modal viewer (`#diagramModal`) for high-resolution exploration of `research-architecture.svg`.
* **Motion Accessibility:** Strict `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rules disable transitions and animations.
